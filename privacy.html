Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Your images

CutoutBG removes backgrounds using an AI model that runs entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them.

What we collect

If you join the Pro waiting list we store your email address to tell you about the launch. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page visits. You can ask us to delete your data at any time: hello@cutoutbg.app.

Third parties

The AI model files are downloaded from Hugging Face and the code from jsDelivr; these services see a standard web request. Payments, when live, will be handled by our payment provider as merchant of record.
