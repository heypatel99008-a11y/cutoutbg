Background remover FAQ

Is CutoutBG really free?

Yes. Remove backgrounds from as many images as you like at standard resolution (up to 1500px), with no sign-up and no watermark. Pro adds full-resolution HD and batch processing.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs inside your browser, so your photo never leaves your device. That makes CutoutBG private by design and great for ID photos, client work and unreleased products.

What is the best remove.bg alternative after December 2026?

remove.bg's standalone site closes on 1 December 2026 and background removal moves into Canva. CutoutBG gives you the same one-click cutout, free, with no account and no credits that expire.

Can I use it for Amazon, Etsy or Shopify product photos?

Yes. Remove the background, switch to a pure white (#FFFFFF) background and download a PNG ready for your listing.

Which image formats work?

JPG, PNG and WEBP. You get a transparent PNG back, or a PNG on white, black or any colour you pick.

Why is the first cutout slower?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 115 MB) and caches it. After that, every cutout starts instantly, even offline.
